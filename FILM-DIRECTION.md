# RetentionLens — Flagship Film Direction

> Production brief for the next version of the 5-second landing-page video. The existing website remains published while the final film is created and tested.

## Concept
**THE WORLD BENEATH THE NUMBERS**: a global business as an interconnected miniature living system, in which RetentionLens reveals an underlying failure and illuminates a better decision. RetentionLens is enterprise decision intelligence, not simply a churn calculator or a warehouse scanner.

## Recommended creation workflow
1. Open [Google Flow](https://labs.google/fx/tools/flow) (Veo) or [Runway](https://runwayml.com/product/) (Gen-4.5).
2. Choose **Text to Video**, ideally 16:9, 1080p or higher, 6–8 seconds. Availability of models and resolutions depends on account access and product settings.
3. Paste the prompt below. Generate multiple takes; choose for convincing motion, art direction and visual consistency.
4. Download the winning MP4. Cut the most polished ~5 seconds. Avoid jumpy transitions; use a short crossfade or a loop-compatible start/end if needed.
5. Save the final file as **assets/retentionlens-film.mp4** in the GitHub repository (replace the old file). Update **assets/retentionlens-poster.jpg** from a clean frame. Keep the `<video autoplay muted loop playsinline>` element intact.
6. Test at https://marvelprince-bit.github.io/retentionlens/ on both desktop and phone with sound muted and a standard internet connection.

## Ready-to-paste generation prompt

CINEMATIC PRODUCT FILM — “THE WORLD BENEATH THE NUMBERS”

Create a premium, photoreal, high-end technology-company launch film, ONE continuous unbroken shot, 16:9 widescreen, 6–8 seconds, physically realistic cinematic CG, 24 fps, subtle anamorphic lens characteristics.

An extraordinary miniature digital twin of a global business floats in an expansive dark environment. It is an interconnected living world, not a computer dashboard: intimate detailed illuminated storefronts, a logistics warehouse, delivery routes, a customer contact center and a financial district are constructed from refined architectural glass and dark metal. Fine streams of light move naturally along their connections.

The camera glides slowly through the environment with elegant, convincing parallax, scale, depth of field and restrained atmospheric haze. A single crimson disturbance appears in a delivery corridor and subtly propagates through the interconnected network. An enormous transparent optical lens passes through the scene, refracting and revealing the true weak link while several alternative routes momentarily become visible. One evidence-supported route begins to glow in cool blue and champagne gold; the disturbance recedes. The world remains alive and in motion, with the camera settling into an elegant stable composition that can repeat as a website hero loop.

Prestige visual language: Apple product cinematography meets architectural visualization meets restrained high-end science fiction. Deep graphite and obsidian blacks, subtle glacial blues, controlled amber-gold highlights, sparing red only for the failure signal. Physically based rendering; exquisite material reflections, true depth, soft cinematic volumetric lighting, sophisticated composition, premium finish. The light has a purpose; no generic random particles.

ABSOLUTELY NO words, letters, logos, numbers, user interfaces, dashboards, charts, people, floating screens, explosions, cartoon effects, rapid cuts or shaky camera. No speech or dialogue. Design for autoplay while muted.

## Acceptance checklist
- The shot has continuous, believable three-dimensional motion, **not** a stitched image slideshow.
- The scene conveys evidence → root cause → options → better action in one coherent visual progression.
- The product identity is precise; no invented business performance statistics or false claims.
- The video is readable behind website text and works when muted.
- The file is compressed for fast web loading, ideally an MP4 H.264 encode with a poster fallback.
- The film is ≈5 seconds in the deployed version and loops automatically on mobile/desktop.
- Both pages and the existing chatbot/X-factor remain functional.
