QUESTION
THE OBVIOUS.

RetentionLens is an evidence-first business decision intelligence concept. Its aim is not simply to generate persuasive answers. It is to help teams identify what they know, confront what they do not, compare competing explanations, and select actions whose value can actually be measured.

[ 01 ] / THE PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE

FOUR LAYERS.
ONE ACCOUNTABLE DECISION.

The long-term design combines independent analysis, economic evaluation, controlled action, and learning. These are architectural goals—not claims that every component is implemented today.

I.

BUSINESS INTELLIGENCE

Build a trusted evidence base. Separate observed metrics from assumptions. Generate independent hypotheses that can be checked against the facts.

EVIDENCE
II.

DECISION INTELLIGENCE

Compare interventions, including doing nothing. Model costs, potential uplift, uncertainty and downside risk. Use adversarial challenge to test conclusions.

CHOICE
III.

VALUE REALIZATION

Keep people in control of approvals. Measure interventions with experiments, holdouts and business outcomes rather than assuming recommendations created value.

OUTCOMES
IV.

ADAPTIVE INTELLIGENCE

Use measured results to calibrate future decisions. Require evaluation and gated promotion before system changes are adopted.

[ 02 ] / WHAT IS ACTUALLY LIVE

PROTOTYPE
NOT PRETENSE.

The current working chatbot is a RetentionLens customer-retention advisor created for an MBA lab. It asks for a business problem, explores potential drivers, distinguishes evidence from hypotheses, and suggests follow-up questions and tests.

The interactive Case Lab on the landing page is a transparent browser-based economic model. It recalculates potential revenue impact as assumptions change; it does not claim to predict outcomes or establish causality.

The broader agentic platform—including independently reasoned hypotheses, adversarial adjudication, governance and adaptive learning—is a development roadmap, not a completed product.
